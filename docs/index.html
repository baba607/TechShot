What is Tech Shot?

Tech Shot is a personal automation tool built and operated by its owner. It turns verified Hindi tech and AI news into short vertical videos (Reels and Shorts) and publishes them — only after the owner's manual review and approval — to the owner's own Instagram account, Facebook Page, and YouTube channel.

The app is used by one person (the owner) for the owner's own channels. It does not serve any other users, collect data from the public, run ads, or track anyone.

How it works

1. Discover — The tool reads public tech-news RSS feeds and official blogs (for example Gadgets360 and official Google announcements).
2. Verify — Every story is checked against its original source and date, with a second source for big stories. A story that cannot be verified is never published.
3. Create — A short Hindi script (30–45 seconds) is written, a Hindi voiceover is generated with the free Kokoro text-to-speech model, and the video is assembled with FFmpeg (1080×1920, subtitles included).
4. Approve & publish — The owner reviews the video and approves it. Only then is it published to Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
